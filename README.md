## Overview

Niall of the Nine Hostages (*Niall Noígíallach*, c. 379–405 AD) was High King of Ireland and progenitor of the Uí Néill dynasty — one of the most enduring and consequential dynasties in Irish and Scottish history. Estimates suggest upward of 3 million men worldwide today carry his Y-chromosome lineage. He raided Roman Britain, held his own family as political hostages to keep the clan from fracturing, and set forces in motion that reshaped the religious and cultural landscape of an entire island.

He has never appeared in a Civilization game. That is a wrong worth righting.

This mod adds Niall as a fully realized, historically grounded leader for the Antiquity Age. He plays unlike anyone in the base game: a dynastic expansionist who grows stronger the more he conquers, whose AI is transactional and ruthless, and whose unique narrative event chain confronts the player with one of history's great unintended consequences — and makes the choice matter permanently.

## Features

### Unique Leader Ability — *Naosc na Naonúir* (Bond of the Nine)
*"A king does not trust. A king ensures."*

Conquering Settlements grants a stacking bonus to all of Niall's existing cities. The Uí Néill dynasty grows stronger through expansion — not just larger. Every conquest tightens the grip on what he already holds.

### Agenda — *Blood of Kings*
Niall respects leaders who take what the world offers. He has no patience for civilizations that build monuments while leaving their borders undefended. His diplomacy reflects it.

### Narrative Event Chain — *A Slave from Across the Water*
A unique, guaranteed event fires once per game in the Antiquity Age, triggered by Niall's first military expansion. A choice is presented. The consequences are permanent — and may echo into Ages you haven't reached yet.

*The slave you dismiss may become the saint who remakes your kingdom.*

### Steam Workshop
*(Link will be added on release.)*

Subscribe on the Workshop and the mod will install automatically. Enable it from the Mods menu on the Civilization VII main screen.

### Manual Install
1. Download or clone this repository
2. Copy the `niall-of-the-nine-hostages/` folder into your Civ VII mods directory:
   - **Windows:** `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Firaxis Games\Sid Meier's Civilization VII\Mods\`
   - **Mac:** `~/Library/Application Support/Sid Meier's Civilization VII/Mods/`
3. Launch Civilization VII and enable the mod from the Mods menu

## Compatibility

- **Game version:** Developed and tested against Civilization VII v1.2.x
- **Age:** Antiquity only — Niall persists across all Ages as leaders do, but his content is designed for and triggers in the Antiquity Age
- **Conflicts:** No known conflicts with base game content. May conflict with other mods that add Celtic civilizations or modify the Antiquity Age narrative event pool — load order may matter; place this mod last if issues arise
- **DLC:** No DLC required

## Design Philosophy

This mod is built on historical record, not fantasy. Niall is not a hero, not a villain — he is a man of his time, utterly pragmatic, who built one of history's most enduring dynasties by being unsentimental about loyalty and ruthless about power.

The narrative event at the heart of this mod is grounded in genuine history. The choice the player faces is the choice Niall actually faced — or something very close to it. The consequences reflect what actually happened. If you pick wrong by Niall's lights, history will tell you so.

The druidic order's folk name — the Black Adders — and their role in the event chain draw on popular tradition and the enduring legend of Patrick driving the snakes from Ireland. Historians rightly note that the snake-as-druid metaphor is a later folk construction rather than a contemporary account, but legends earn their place in the story of a people regardless of their pedigree. This is a game that lets you rewrite history. Using its best legends feels appropriate.
